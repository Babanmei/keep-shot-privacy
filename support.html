FAQ

Photo permission denied / 照片权限被拒

Go to Settings → Keep Shot on your iPhone and allow photo access, then reopen the app.
请在 iPhone 的「设置 → Keep Shot」中允许照片访问，再重新打开应用。

Storage unavailable / 存储空间不足

Free up space on the destination (iPhone or external drive) and try the backup again.
请清理目标位置（iPhone 或外接磁盘）的空间后重试备份。

Backup interrupted / 备份中断

Already-transferred files are not duplicated. Reconnect and resume — the app continues from where it left off.
已传输的文件不会重复传输。重新连接后续传即可，应用会从中断处继续。

Verification failed / 验证失败

If a file fails hash verification, it is never deleted from your library. Re-run the backup for that item.
若文件哈希校验失败，它不会被删除。请对该项目重新备份。

Delete failed / 删除失败

Some photos could not be deleted. Wait a moment and retry; items that failed stay in your library.
部分照片无法删除时，请稍后重试，未成功删除的会保留在相册中。

How do I recover cleaned photos? / 如何恢复已清理的照片？

Cleaned photos go to the iPhone "Recently Deleted" album and can be recovered within 30 days.
被清理的照片会进入 iPhone 的「最近删除」相簿，30 天内可恢复。

How does backup work? / 备份如何工作？

Copy → Verify (hash) → Delete. The app always verifies a file before removing the original, so your photos are never lost.
流程为：复制 → 校验（哈希）→ 删除。应用始终先校验再删除原文件，确保照片不丢失。
