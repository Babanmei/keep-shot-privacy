Keep Shot
Privacy Policy · 隐私政策
Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Overview

Keep Shot is an iOS app that helps you organize your photo library and free up storage by finding duplicate, similar, blurry, and unwanted photos. Your privacy is central to how the app is built: by default, all analysis happens on your device, and your photos are not uploaded to any server.

1. 概述
Keep Shot 是一款 iOS 相册整理工具，用于发现重复、相似、模糊及不需要的照片，帮你释放存储空间。隐私是我们的设计核心：默认情况下，所有分析都在你的设备上进行，你的照片不会上传到任何服务器。

2. Information We Collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or location. The only user data the app accesses is your Photos and Videos, used solely to provide the app's functionality (finding duplicates, similar shots, blur, etc.). This data is not linked to you and is not used for tracking.

2. 我们收集的信息
我们不需注册账号，也不收集姓名、邮箱或位置等个人信息。应用唯一访问的用户数据是你的照片与视频，仅用于实现应用功能（查找重复、相似、模糊照片等）。这些数据不与你关联，也不用于跟踪。

3. On-Device Processing

Scanning, perceptual hashing, similarity grouping, blur detection, and best-photo ranking run locally on your iPhone. Photo files are read only to analyze them; they are never sent off the device unless you explicitly enable Cloud AI (see below).

3. 设备端处理
扫描、感知哈希、相似分组、模糊检测与最佳照片排序均在 iPhone 本地运行。照片仅被读取以做分析，不会离开设备，除非你主动开启云端 AI（见下）。

4. Optional Cloud AI (Pro)

Cloud AI is an optional feature. When you turn it on, only the content you choose to analyze is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the AI provider you configure. We do not store your photos, and the app is designed so that API keys are never embedded in the app bundle.

4. 可选的云端 AI（Pro）
云端 AI 为可选功能。开启后，仅你选择分析的内容会通过加密的 HTTPS 连接发送到你自行配置的 AI 服务商。我们不存储你的照片，且应用设计上不会把 API Key 写入安装包。

5. Deletion & Backup

When you clean photos, they are moved to the iPhone "Recently Deleted" album and remain recoverable for 30 days — they are never permanently deleted without your explicit choice. The optional backup feature copies photos to a location you choose (Mac, PC, or external SSD) and verifies each file with a hash before any removal.

5. 删除与备份
清理照片时，它们会被移入 iPhone 的「最近删除」相簿，30 天内可恢复——除非你明确选择，否则绝不永久删除。可选的备份功能会把照片复制到你选择的位置（Mac / PC / 外接 SSD），并在删除前逐张做哈希校验。

6. Data Retention

Because analysis is on-device and we operate no user-photo servers, we do not retain copies of your photos. Local analysis results (hashes, scores) are stored only in the app's private sandbox on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

6. 数据保留
由于分析在设备端完成、我们也未运营存储用户照片的服务器，我们不保留你的照片副本。本地的分析结果（哈希、评分）仅存于应用私有沙盒，卸载应用后即被清除。

7. Children

The app is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. 儿童
本应用评级为 4+，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集任何儿童的数据。

8. Your Rights

As we do not store your photos on our servers, you can exercise your rights simply by deleting the app. For any question about this policy, contact us below.

8. 你的权利
由于我们不在服务器上存储你的照片，你只需卸载应用即可行使相关权利。对本政策有任何疑问，请通过下方方式联系我们。

9. Changes

9. 变更
