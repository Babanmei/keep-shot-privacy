Keep Shot
AI keeps your best photos, safely backs up the rest, and frees up space.

Keep Shot uses on-device AI to help you keep only the photos worth keeping. It finds duplicates, similar shots, blurry images, and screenshots you no longer need — then recommends the best photo in each group so you decide what to clean. All analysis runs locally; your photos are not uploaded by default.

Privacy by design
