System requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 15.0 or later.
  • Free space on the device to run the on-device scan (a few hundred MB recommended).
  • Photo library access granted in Settings → Keep Shot.
  • For the optional backup feature: a Mac, PC, or external SSD with enough free space for your photos.

Getting started

  1. Open Keep Shot and grant full photo library access when prompted.
  2. Tap Scan — analysis runs entirely on your device; no photos are uploaded.
  3. Review the groups (Duplicates, Similar, Blurry, Screenshots, Large Videos).
  4. For each group, Keep Shot recommends the best photo and explains why (sharpness, expression, composition).
  5. Confirm to clean. Cleaned photos move to Recently Deleted on your iPhone and stay recoverable for 30 days.

How cleaning works (safe by design)

When you clean photos, they are moved to the iPhone “Recently Deleted” album and remain recoverable for 30 days. They are never permanently deleted without your explicit choice.

The optional backup follows a strict order: Copy → Verify (hash) → Delete. The app always verifies a file with a hash before removing the original, so your photos are never lost.

Backup to Mac / PC / external SSD

  1. Connect your destination drive or computer.
  2. Choose Backup & Clean in Keep Shot.
  3. Each photo is copied, then hash-verified against the original.
  4. Only after verification succeeds is the original moved to Recently Deleted.

If a transfer is interrupted, already-transferred files are not duplicated — reconnect and resume, and the app continues from where it left off.

Troubleshooting

Photo permission denied

Go to Settings → Keep Shot on your iPhone, allow photo access, then reopen the app.

Storage unavailable

Free up space on the destination (iPhone or external drive) and try the backup again.

Backup interrupted

Already-transferred files are not duplicated. Reconnect and resume — the app continues from where it left off.

Verification failed

If a file fails hash verification, it is never deleted from your library. Re-run the backup for that item.

Delete failed

Some photos could not be deleted. Wait a moment and retry; items that failed stay in your library.

How do I recover cleaned photos?

Cleaned photos go to the iPhone “Recently Deleted” album and can be recovered within 30 days via the Photos app.

Privacy note

Keep Shot processes everything on your device. Your photos are not uploaded by default. Cloud AI (Pro) is optional and sends only the content you choose to the AI provider you configure. Read the full policy: Privacy Policy.
